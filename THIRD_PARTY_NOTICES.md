## FFmpeg

The production container builds FFmpeg 8.1.2 from the official source archive with
`--disable-gpl`, `--disable-nonfree`, and `--disable-autodetect`. The resulting executable is
used as a separate command-line process and is distributed under LGPL-2.1-or-later. The matching
LGPL text is installed at `/usr/share/licenses/ffmpeg/COPYING.LGPLv2.1` in the runtime image.
FFmpeg source is available from <https://ffmpeg.org/download.html>.

The local developer machine may have a differently configured FFmpeg installation. Only the
container-built LGPL configuration is approved for production deployment.
